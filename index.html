A guest post by University of Chicago number theorist Frank Calegari on Terence Tao's blog. Calegari argues that now AI can close famous open problems, picking the right problem matters more than producing the proof, and he lists six changes the field should make: evaluate all work as if it were AI-assisted and judge it on understanding and consequences rather than the result; hire on interviews and mathematical vision instead of paper counts; raise journal standards or drop journals entirely; widen graduate curricula past narrow technical training; run seminars around fundamental questions rather than incremental novelty; and claim AI-produced results as part of mathematics' collective knowledge instead of crediting the labs. He cites the 1983 classification of finite simple groups as a case where solving the famous problem drained interest from the field, and sketches cross-area work linking arithmetic hyperbolic three-manifolds and Kolyvagin systems that he says would be impractical without AI to synthesize the background.

芝加哥大学数论学家 Frank Calegari 在陶哲轩博客上的客座文章。他认为既然 AI 已能攻克著名未解问题，选对问题就比给出证明更重要，并提出六项改革：把所有工作都当作有 AI 协助来评价，依据理解与后续影响而非结果本身；以面试和数学眼光取代论文数量来招聘；提高期刊标准或干脆弃用期刊；拓宽研究生课程，不再局限于狭窄的技术训练；研讨会围绕根本问题而非增量新意；把 AI 产出的结果视为数学界的共同知识，而不是归功于那些实验室。他以 1983 年有限单群分类为例，说明攻克著名问题反而抽走了该领域的兴趣，并勾画了一项连接算术双曲三维流形与 Kolyvagin 系统的跨领域研究，称若无 AI 整合背景知识则难以推进。
