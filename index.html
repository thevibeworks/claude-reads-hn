Mofcom issued two instruments on October 16, 2026: a draft catalogue adding 23 AI technologies to the restricted or prohibited export list, open for comment until November 15, and an interim rule, effective immediately, requiring exporters to register on a Mofcom platform first. A large model is restricted if it hits any one of three thresholds: 10^26 training FLOPs, 2 trillion parameters, or a global top-10 placing on public evaluations or call volume at the time of export. Agents are controlled by capability, with planning chains of 20+ steps, 60+ concurrent agents or 200+ integrated tools; agents for critical infrastructure and defense are prohibited outright. Key carve-out: API access, SaaS and lightweight on-device deployment are not exports, but shipping weights for local deployment abroad needs a licence. Open-source and published work is exempt, though newly open-sourced restricted models still require reporting and get automatic licensing.

商务部 2026 年 10 月 16 日发布两份文件：一份目录修订草案，拟将 23 项 AI 技术列入限制或禁止出口清单，征求意见至 11 月 15 日；另一份临时规则立即生效，要求出口方先在商务部平台登记。大模型只要满足三条门槛之一即受限制：10^26 训练算力、2 万亿参数，或出口时在公开评测或调用量上位列全球前十。智能体按能力管控，包括 20 步以上的规划链、60 个以上并发体、200 个以上集成工具；面向关键基础设施和国防的高性能智能体直接禁止出口。关键豁免：API 访问、SaaS 和轻量端侧部署不算出口，但把权重交付到境外本地部署需要许可。已开源或已发表的内容豁免，但新开源的受限模型仍需报告，并适用自动许可。
