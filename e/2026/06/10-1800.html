Instead of predicting words one at a time like a typewriter, it drafts an entire 256-token paragraph simultaneously - like a printing press stamping the whole page at once. This utilizes your hardware to full potential and achieves 4x faster generation. NVIDIA is hosting a free endpoint for testing.
