Clytia jellyfish heal wounds in minutes without scarring by using two mechanisms in sequence: lamellipodia (foot-like actin extensions) crawl across the basement membrane to drag cells forward, then actomyosin cables contract to close the gap. Even single-cell wounds use the same process. Researcher Jocelyn Malamy says the mechanisms are conserved across species, so insights may apply to mammals.
