Solod (So) is a strict subset of Go that compiles to readable C11. Zero runtime, no GC, no hidden allocations. Stack-allocated by default with opt-in heap. Supports structs, methods, interfaces, slices, maps, defer, and limited generics. Uses standard Go tooling (LSP, linting, testing). Currently v0.2 with networking and WebAssembly support, v0.3 adding concurrency.

Solod (So) 是 Go 的严格子集，编译为可读的 C11。零运行时，无 GC，无隐藏分配。默认栈分配，堆分配可选。支持结构体、方法、接口、切片、映射、defer 和有限泛型。使用标准 Go 工具（LSP、linting、测试）。目前 v0.2 支持网络和 WebAssembly，v0.3 将添加并发。
