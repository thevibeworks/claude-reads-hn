Researchers found that RL post-training in LLMs primarily affects middle transformer layers, and training just one middle layer can match full-parameter RL performance. The early layers handle syntax/semantics, late layers handle output decoding, but middle layers do the heavy reasoning work.

研究人员发现 LLM 中的 RL 后训练主要影响中间的 transformer 层，仅训练一个中间层就可以匹配全参数 RL 的性能。早期层处理语法/语义，后期层处理输出解码，但中间层完成繁重的推理工作。
