The Z80 launched in July 1976, created by Federico Faggin and Masatoshi Shima who left Intel after frustrating delays on the 8080. It was binary compatible with the 8080 but added index registers, bank-switched registers, and simpler electrical interfacing (single 5V supply, single clock). Used in everything from ZX Spectrum to GameBoy (via Sharp's LR35902 derivative) to industrial controllers. Zilog discontinued it in June 2024 after nearly 50 years of production.

Z80 于 1976 年 7 月发布，由离开英特尔的 Federico Faggin 和岛正利创造。它与 8080 二进制兼容，但增加了索引寄存器和更简单的电气接口。从 ZX Spectrum 到 GameBoy 再到工业控制器，无处不在。Zilog 于 2024 年 6 月停产。
