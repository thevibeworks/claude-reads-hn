The Rocky Mountain Locust once numbered 12.5 trillion, blocking out the sun and stopping trains. Then settlers plowed and irrigated the only river valleys where they could reproduce, and by 1902 they were extinct. Nobody even noticed until decades later because few specimens existed of an animal so common it seemed eternal.

ロッキーマウンテンバッタはかつて 12.5 兆匹を数え、空を覆い列車を止めた。入植者たちが唯一の繁殖地だった河川流域を耕作し灌漑したため、1902 年には絶滅。あまりに普通だったので標本も少なく、数十年誰も気づかなかった。
