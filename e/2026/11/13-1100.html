Edge0 released Edge0-145B, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with about 10B active parameters per step, aimed at coding and agent work on personal computers. Each group of 64 experts shares two large matrices, and each expert keeps a small set of its own parameters. Hot experts stay in GPU memory, a roughly 40GB expert library sits in RAM and about 48GB of vocabulary data is read from SSD on demand, for a reported 29GB peak memory. Self-reported scores: 86.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 64.6 on DeepSWE, ahead of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next; most numbers come from internal evals and the competitor memory figures are estimates.
