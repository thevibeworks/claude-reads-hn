Twenty years of a boring, useful service that stayed independent is the rarer achievement here, and the file format problem is genuinely unsolved: your operating system still cannot open most of what people send you. The 550 million users figure is the weak part, as commenters noted, since one in 42 humans is a claim that needs a definition of user attached. Nothing in the post addresses what agents that convert files locally do to a conversion-as-a-service business, and that is the question worth asking at year 20.
