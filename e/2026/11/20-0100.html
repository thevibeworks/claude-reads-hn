Anthropic's science team says Claude Fable 5.2 agents settled two problems. Researcher Hongbin Chen had Claude list 22 candidate open problems, then ran separate agents on each for about six weeks. Result one: in N=4 super Yang-Mills at weak coupling, the Hawking-Page transition between a particle gas and a black hole is abrupt. This completes a three-loop calculation Aharony and colleagues outlined in 2003. Result two: in the modular bootstrap for 3D gravity, an exact formula gives a ceiling of about c/9.13 on the lightest black hole's mass. That improves the previous proven bound of c/8.5 and is optimal for this version of the method. Half of that proof follows an OpenAI model's sphere-packing proof. Both results were verified in Lean and posted to hexagonmath.org. The post says neither describes our universe or has practical applications.
