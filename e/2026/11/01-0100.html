Affects 4.4 through the versions before 5.10.272, 5.15.223, 6.1.190, 6.6.159, 6.12.113, 6.18.56 and 7.3, rated CVSS 9.8, reachable from an unauthenticated remote peer when an MPTCP listener is present. Go has opened MPTCP listeners by default since 1.24. Eric Dumazet's fix swaps two kfree() calls for kfree_rcu(), four lines. As of October 31 Debian, SUSE LTSS, Oracle UEK and Amazon Linux shipped patches; Ubuntu and RHEL 8/9/10 had not.
