A four-page note from mathematician Levent Alpöge says that, working with Claude, he has a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis that extends to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, and possibly later to the Extended one. He writes that Claude did much of the technical work near the end and that he is not an expert in the automorphic-forms machinery involved. The approach adapts Stepanov's function-field method to the integers, getting the needed multiplicity from a chain of roughly sqrt(X) iterated theta lifts between unitary groups. The only artifact released is a Lean formalization split into 14 archive parts. A draft write-up passed 250,000 pages, and he declined to publish an unreadable version. The note flags steps still needing work, including a weakened form of Vandiver's conjecture. No independent review is reported. A separate post on the math216 blog the same day announced a different proof by Alessandro Masullo.
