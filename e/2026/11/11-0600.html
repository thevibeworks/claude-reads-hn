Federally funded researchers must buy synthetic nucleic acids and benchtop synthesizers only from US-approved suppliers, who have to verify customer identity and screen orders of 50 base pairs or longer against sequences encoding toxins and pathogens. The policy binds federally funded labs, which are the places that already have oversight, and leaves everyone else to a voluntary decision by vendors. Experts expect synthesis companies to screen all customers anyway, and that expectation is doing the real enforcement work here, which is a strange place for a bioterrorism rule to put its weight. King-Aaron's objection in the thread lands: a terrorist is not a federal grantee. The 50-base-pair floor and the exemption for synthesizers already on benches are the parts worth arguing about.
