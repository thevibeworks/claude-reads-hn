Early automobiles needed constant repair. Wealthy owners replaced their deferential coachmen with mechanics who had 'egalitarian instincts' and zero respect for social hierarchy. These chauffeurs went joyriding, skimmed money, and held power because they alone could fix the complex machines. By end of WWI, cars got reliable and owners learned to drive - shifting power dynamics. Same pattern repeats: typists to word processors, file clerks to computers.
