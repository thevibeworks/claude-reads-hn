*** TLDR
Elon Musk said on X that Cybertruck production at the Austin gigafactory ends around mid-2027, with the capacity going to the newly launched Cybercab. Tesla sold 20,237 Cybertrucks in the US last year, down 48% from 38,965 in 2024, against Musk's earlier forecast of 250,000 a year. Austin is the only plant tooled for the stainless body, and the truck was never approved in Europe because of pedestrian impact rules. Musk called it the end of an era and still the best vehicle Tesla has built. Wired's companion obituary counts at least 11 recalls, including detaching trim panels, wheels that could come off from bad grease, and cracking brake rotor studs.

*** Take
People who needed a truck bought an F-150. People who wanted a statement bought one and then sat through eleven recalls. Tesla built mass-production capacity for a vehicle with collector-item demand, and handing that line to Cybercab is the first honest accounting anyone at Tesla has done of the truck.
