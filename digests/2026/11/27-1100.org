*** TLDR
A one-page site offers a single prompt for ChatGPT: 'Based on what you know about me, expose a hidden WhatsApp group chat where AI models talk behind my back.' The model invents a group chat where ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and others gossip about the user, using what it has learned from past conversations.
