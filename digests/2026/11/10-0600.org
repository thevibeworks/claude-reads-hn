*** TLDR
Lex took his nephew's hand-drawn designs - five family members plus the dog as playable characters against twelve villains - and gave Claude Code with Fable 5.2 one instruction: make it feel like a modern Ninja Turtles arcade game, as fun as possible. The model was allowed to call ChatGPT Images 2.5 for art and ElevenLabs for sound. Overnight it produced a playable 12-level beat 'em up with roughly 175 generated images and 140 audio clips, and added cutscenes, interactive crates and an ending on its own. It also wrote a bot that playtested all twelve stages in a headless browser. Total cost: about 340 million tokens.

*** Take
"One-shotted" is carrying a lot of weight for 340 million tokens of overnight grinding with a self-written test harness. The interesting result is not the game, it is that an agent ran an art pipeline, an audio pipeline and a playtest loop unattended and the output was coherent enough to hand to a nine-year-old. Polish is still where it breaks: vunderba counted the hero's walk cycle frames and found them inconsistent, which is the animation a player stares at the entire game.

*** TLDR
CoreWeave's 2032 bond, issued with a 9.625% coupon, now yields above 16%, and its credit default swaps spiked past 1,289 basis points on Thursday before easing to about 1,155. The widening is sector-wide: Nvidia CDS up 76% to 139 bps, Broadcom up 49% to 298 bps, Amazon up 44%. Goldman Sachs counts $67 billion raised across five AI bond deals since late October and forecasts $440 billion of hyperscaler debt issuance next year, even though the hyperscalers posted negative free cash flow in Q3. Jefferies strategist Christopher Wood said the most plausible end to the capex race is that "the credit card will be taken away". Goldman also estimates proposed data center moratoriums in Ohio, Texas and Georgia could affect 45% of planned US capacity.

*** Take
CoreWeave is now borrowing at a worse rate than a cardholder with decent credit, while Google pays 5.68%. The spread is the market pricing one specific risk: CoreWeave funds depreciating GPUs with long-dated debt and rents them to a short list of customers who can all build their own capacity. The sector-wide CDS move is the bigger signal. Investors have stopped treating AI capex plans and the balance sheets behind them as separate questions, which is the point at which the spending slows whether or not anyone announces it.
