*** TLDR
Luke Sernau, listed as working at Anthropic, published a Lean 4 formalization of the Riemann Hypothesis and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions. It is based on Levent Alpöge's Claude-assisted proof, which ran to about 17M lines. The repo has two editions: a readable one of about 300k lines with a guide, and a compact one of about 76k lines covering the same five theorems. Both use only Lean's standard axioms. The proof is machine-checked only, with no full human review and no paper, and the README does not say how the shortening was done. A build takes 1-2 hours on 12 cores and 24 GB of RAM.

*** Take
A 99.5% cut in days, while a community effort with hundreds of contributors got 33%, means the original 17M lines were mostly unpruned agent output. The 300k edition matters more than the 76k headline number, because it is the first version a mathematician could realistically read. Anthropic has not announced any of it: the work arrives as a personal repo with an Anthropic copyright line.

*** TLDR
Security researcher Hanno Böck posted to oss-security that Ubuntu is days to weeks behind on critical kernel fixes. NoisyTCP (CVE-2026-99288), a remote memory corruption bug in the TCP stack, was published November 16 and Ubuntu still had no fix nine days later, though other distros shipped at disclosure. A KVM host escape on AMD (CVE-2026-99312) from November 18 was also unfixed. An earlier remote TCP bug (CVE-2026-98432), possibly exploited in the wild, took Ubuntu several weeks. Böck blames Ubuntu's practice of backporting fixes onto its own 7.0 kernel instead of tracking kernel.org longterm releases, and says remote kernel corruption bugs should be fixed within hours.

*** Take
Ubuntu runs its own kernel branch, so every critical fix needs a custom backport and QA across many kernel flavors (Azure, AWS, HWE). With disclosure rates this high, that model can't keep up, and paying support customers in the thread say they get no faster answers. Red Hat is only slightly better, which suggests the backport model itself is the problem.
