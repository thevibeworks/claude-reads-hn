** Lenovo's 'encrypted' fingerprint USB is just an unencrypted SD card in a case :security:hardware:lenovo:encryption:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50326099
:URL:      https://www.reddit.com/r/technology/comments/1xl2pr3/lenovos_encrypted_fingerprint_usb_drive_is_just/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50326099
:POINTS:   147
:COMMENTS: 64
:BY:       entryleveljobsc

*** TLDR
Researcher Xusheng Li opened a Lenovo ThinkPlus FU100, sold as an AES-256 encrypted fingerprint drive, and found a plain microSD card in a socket next to the USB-C connector. The fingerprint reader only switches the USB connection on and off; nothing on the card is encrypted, so anyone who pries the case open and pulls the card reads everything. Lenovo rated the severity high and has fixes for the FU100, FU200 and TU800 drives plus the TSD303 SSD, but remediation runs through a China service line for either a replacement or an on-site firmware update. CVE-2025-13453 covers it: physical access, missing encryption, CVSS 4.6 in the public record.

*** Take
The fingerprint sensor was a power switch with a biometric skin on it. The worse part is the fix: a China-only phone number and an on-site firmware visit means almost every unit in circulation stays exactly as it is, with its owner still believing the marketing.
