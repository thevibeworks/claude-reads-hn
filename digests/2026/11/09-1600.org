*** TLDR
Anthropic released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on November 9. Same underlying model: Fable ships with cybersecurity and biology safeguards and goes to all users, Mythos drops them for organizations vetted through the Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Pricing is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, with generation up to 30% faster than 5.1; an optional fast mode doubles the price for up to 2.5x speed. Headline numbers: 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 at xhigh effort, 89.6% CVE rediscovery, 64.9% on RoboDojo, 85.1% protein design. A team of 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents fully formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours, seven times shorter than an unreleased research version's 11-day run. Available on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure.

*** Take
The release landed a day after the "Opus 5.5 is nerfed" thread hit the front page, which is the worst possible sequencing even if the two things are unrelated. And breakwaterlabs' benchmark questions deserve an answer: stacking Fable xHigh against Carbon Med, then switching the comparison to Argon for robotics, is the kind of chart-shopping that makes 49.1% read as arbitrary rather than impressive.

*** TLDR
Payments Canada switched on the Real-Time Rail on November 9, giving the country 24/7 clearing and settlement over ISO 20022 messaging. Eight institutions are live on day one: Wealthsimple, Questbank, Peoples Trust, Paramount Commerce, KOHO, DoBusiness.com, Digital Commerce Bank and Bank of America.
