** Valkey kills the skiplist, cuts sorted set overhead by up to 43% :databases:valkey:redis:datastructures:performance:

:BY:       reconditerose

*** TLDR
Valkey 9.2, now in release-candidate testing, replaces the skiplist behind large sorted sets with fbtree, a B+ tree with 61-way fanout and 512-byte leaves sized to jemalloc classes. Score and member are packed into one value, inner nodes keep a 4-byte 'feature' per child that SIMD compares in parallel, and leaves are doubly linked for range scans. Each skiplist node cost about 37 bytes before the member string. With 5M 20-byte members, per-member index overhead fell from 50.3B to 28.5B for sequential inserts (43%) and to 32.0B for random inserts (36%). On a 3M-member set, ZADD score updates got 105% faster, ZREM 76%, ZCOUNT 27% and ZRANK 19%; hashtable-backed commands like ZSCORE barely moved. Commands are unchanged, but OBJECT ENCODING now reports 'btree' instead of 'skiplist'. Small listpack sets are unaffected.

*** Take
The gains land where you would expect: ZADD and ZREM, which move elements around, improved the most, and lookups that go through the hashtable did not change. The benchmarks come from the PR branch on a 64-core Graviton3 machine and were not rerun on the merged commit, so measure your own workload on the RC as the authors suggest. The one change likely to break something is OBJECT ENCODING returning 'btree', so check any monitoring script that matches the string 'skiplist'. The thread spent more time arguing over whether the blog post and PR description were LLM-written than on the data structure.

***** Title
Valkey 弃用跳表，有序集合开销最多降低 43%
***** TLDR
正在进行候选版本测试的 Valkey 9.2 用 fbtree 取代了大型有序集合背后的跳表。fbtree 是一种 B+ 树，扇出为 61，叶节点 512 字节，按 jemalloc 分配级别设计。分数和成员被打包成一个值，内部节点为每个子节点保存一个 4 字节的“特征”，可用 SIMD 并行比较，叶节点双向链接以便范围扫描。每个跳表节点在成员字符串之外约占 37 字节。在 500 万个 20 字节成员的测试中，顺序插入时每成员索引开销从 50.3B 降到 28.5B（43%），随机插入时降到 32.0B（36%）。在 300 万成员的集合上，ZADD 分数更新快了 105%，ZREM 快 76%，ZCOUNT 快 27%，ZRANK 快 19%；ZSCORE 等走哈希表的命令几乎没变。命令行为不变，但 OBJECT ENCODING 现在返回 'btree' 而不是 'skiplist'。小型 listpack 集合不受影响。
***** Take
提升出现在预期的地方：需要移动元素的 ZADD 和 ZREM 提升最大，走哈希表的查找没有变化。基准测试来自 PR 分支，运行在 64 核 Graviton3 机器上，没有在合并后的提交上重跑，所以应按作者建议在 RC 上测自己的负载。最可能出问题的是 OBJECT ENCODING 返回 'btree'，检查一下匹配 'skiplist' 字符串的监控脚本。评论区争论博客和 PR 描述是否由 LLM 撰写的时间，比讨论数据结构本身还多。

El método de tono hace una prueba de nivel en nueve frecuencias de 1 a 14 kHz, reproduce sonidos de prueba 14 dB por encima de tu umbral de audición, acota la coincidencia con valoraciones y comparaciones de dos opciones, comprueba una octava por encima y por debajo porque la gente suele equivocarse por una octava, y termina con un ajuste independiente con un deslizador. El resultado es un rango de al menos media octava. Una app de la barra de menús de Mac aplica la muesca a todo el audio del sistema. La web dice que no es un dispositivo médico, no cita estudios concretos y pide acudir al médico ante pérdida súbita de audición, sonido en un solo oído, sonido pulsátil o mareos.
***** Take
