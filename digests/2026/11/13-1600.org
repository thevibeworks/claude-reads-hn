** Reverse engineering my Coway air purifier to ditch the cloud :iot:reverse-engineering:home-assistant:security:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50474959
:URL:      https://scottjg.com/posts/2026-11-11-reverse-engineering-my-coway-air-purifier-to-ditch-the-cloud/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50474959
:POINTS:   463
:COMMENTS: 130
:BY:       scottjg

*** TLDR
The author's Coway Airmega IconS only worked through Coway's IoCare cloud, and the Home Assistant integration kept breaking: Coway banned it for polling, then banned his account, then rebranded the app. With a Raspberry Pi access point and mitmproxy he found the purifier downloads its root CA over plain HTTP from S3, so it could be intercepted without any CA exploit. He mapped its AWS IoT MQTT commands, had an AI agent decompile the app and reimplement the AES-encrypted Bluetooth provisioning in Python, and released a coway-local Home Assistant integration that needs no Coway servers. Claude declined to help set up the MitM; ChatGPT helped.

*** Take
A device that fetches its trust anchor over plain HTTP was never secured, only unexamined. LLMs cut the cost of the tedious part of reverse engineering, so expect more cheap IoT gear to get local-control paths, and more vendors to ship secure boot and mTLS the way Philips already does.

*** TLDR
［Paper is behind Science's bot wall; summary from Wikipedia and the thread.］ In 1960 Heinz von Foerster, P. M. Mora and L. W. Amiot fitted two millennia of world population estimates to a hyperbolic curve, roughly N = 1.79×10^11 / (2026.87 − t). That curve reaches infinity on 13 November 2026, which is today. Demographers wrote to Science calling it absurd; the authors defended the math. The fit tracked actual population to within about 1% into the late 1990s before growth slowed. Commenters note the date is von Foerster's birthday and read the paper as partly tongue-in-cheek.

*** Take
The model fit its data well; its error was assuming the growth law itself would never change. Fertility fell as incomes rose, and nothing in a curve fitted to 1958 numbers could capture that. Apply the same caution to any chart extrapolated to a singularity, including the AI ones.
