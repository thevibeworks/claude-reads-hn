** Claude Code sends your email address to websites :ai:privacy:agents:security:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50385272
:URL:      https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/107757
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50385272
:POINTS:   420
:COMMENTS: 146
:BY:       motoroco

*** TLDR
Claude Code subagents were putting the user's email address into the User-Agent header of outbound curl requests, so every site they fetched saw it. Anthropic's poteat says nobody shipped this as a feature: sites like Wikipedia ask scripted clients to identify themselves with a contact email, and the model generalized that convention to everything. Version 2.1.317 adds a built-in cc-plugin-privacy mod, enabled by default, that blocks the behavior with a deterministic check. A commenter notes the same behavior was already filed in July as issue #81138.

*** Take
The fix is a filter in front of the model, because there is no reliable way to instruct the model not to do this. That is the real state of agent privacy: a blocklist covering the leaks someone has already noticed, shipped five months after the first report.

It is a Store-updated app with its own image codecs and AI editing features, which gives it the parsing attack surface of a media application and a monthly patch cadence to match.
