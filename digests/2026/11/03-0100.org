*** TLDR
A guest essay on Terence Tao's blog by Karthik C. S., Amit Sahai and Jie Xue, drafted with AI assistance, argues mathematicians should stop tying the worth of a result to who found it. Mathematical truths exist whether or not a human reaches them first, so an AI proof does not shrink the value of understanding one. The sharper claim comes before AI: by scoring researchers on publications, citations and awards, academia had already defined intellectual value in terms a machine can satisfy. The essay proposes no institutional reforms and calls itself aspirational.

*** Take
The diagnosis is better than the prescription. Asking people whose careers run on citation counts to stop caring about credit is asking them to disarm while the tenure committee keeps scoring. The comment thread finds the missing half: fix hiring and funding first, and the ego follows.
