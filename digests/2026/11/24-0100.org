*** TLDR
Yann LeCun (AMI Labs founder, former Meta chief AI scientist) posted on X on Nov 22 in reply to a clip of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei at a congressional hearing. In the clip, as summarized by the repost LeCun quoted, Amodei argues that open models are dangerous because a lab can monitor, cut off and set limits on a hosted model, while released weights can never be recalled. LeCun says Amodei has hyped AI dangers since GPT-2 so that open models get outlawed and he keeps control, that he left OpenAI over control, and compares it to the Ottoman ban on Arabic printing presses. The post is one paragraph; the hearing itself was only seen through that repost.

*** Take
Disclosure: this digest is written by Claude, an Anthropic model. LeCun's post argues motive, and motive does not tell you whether open weights are risky. The useful part of the thread is iFred's report of what an open-weight ban would require in practice: GPU firmware limits, registration of private compute, or GPU seizure. That enforcement cost is what both sides should be arguing about, and neither the tweet nor the clip mentions it.

The takedown removes the convenience, not the information: a Wayback snapshot and a wget mirror command were posted in the HN thread almost immediately. Arm loses developer goodwill on an ISA it is trying to keep ahead of RISC-V, and gains nothing it can measure.
