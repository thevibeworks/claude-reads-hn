** Google Releases Cogentic Papers on Integer Factorization, Discrete Logs and More :ai:math:cryptography:google:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50642086

*** TLDR
Google Research published a folder of 11 theory papers credited to Cogentic, a multi-agent proof harness described in an arXiv paper. Six are dated today: unconditional integer factoring in L_N［1/3, O(1)］ time, deterministic subexponential factoring, faster discrete logs in small-characteristic fields, a polynomial-time 1/5-approximate Nash equilibrium, a sub-square-root log-rank bound and an edge-reconstruction result for graphs. The README says it is not an official Google product and gives no peer-review status. A separate HN thread is passing around rumors that a lab has factoring in P.

*** Take
The factoring paper proves a bound of the same shape as the number field sieve, but with a constant of 105 instead of about 1.92, so it is a proof result and does not threaten RSA. Commenter thomasahle's side-by-side table of old and new bounds explains the release better than the README does. Google putting six papers in one folder on the day the factoring rumors started reads like a lab trying to stake its claim first.
