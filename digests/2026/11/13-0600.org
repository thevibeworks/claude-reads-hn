*** TLDR
After Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO, employees filled an internal suggestion-box post started by a longtime engineer with more than 1,600 comments asking the board to bring him back, and about two dozen posted support on LinkedIn. Huang acknowledged in an email that he had been unfaithful to his wife; reporting describes relationships with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset, a direct report and another employee, and the FBI and members of Congress have opened inquiries. The board accepted the resignation before its own investigation finished, named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and installed investor Harvey Jones as chair. Nvidia has lost roughly $1 trillion in market value since the allegations surfaced.

*** Take
The petition is about compensation as much as loyalty. Employees paid largely in Nvidia stock just watched a trillion dollars disappear, and the fastest theoretical route back is the man who left. But the board moved before closing its investigation, which it would not do over an affair alone. A CEO whose relationship ran through a company accused of routing restricted chips is a legal problem, not a morale problem, and no suggestion-box thread resolves it.

*** TLDR
Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast cheaper model for everyday coding, and Orchestrator, a router that switches models inside a single task. Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, just past Argon's 50, and 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 against 3.8 Flash's 18.2%, with four thinking levels including a new xhigh tier. Orchestrator leaves planning and complex reasoning to Carbon and hands execution and edits to Hydrogen; Google claims near-Carbon success rates on Terminal-Bench 4.1 while using 24% less quota and finishing 19% faster. Hydrogen goes to paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, Orchestrator (Carbon) to Ultra and Orchestrator (Argon) to Pro. No pricing was published.

*** Take
Orchestrator's numbers are an admission that Carbon is too slow and too expensive for most of what people actually do with it, so Google built a dispatcher instead of a better flagship. Sound engineering, confusing product: Pro and Ultra now get different routers over different models, and the element names carry no information about which is which. The benchmark jump over 3.8 Flash is the real news, and the naming scheme is the toll you pay to read it.
