*** TLDR
The Tor Project released tor 0.4.9.17 on November 13 as a security release. The announcement singles out one fix to the relay denial-of-service defenses related to IPv6 handling, without describing the bug. The network is under a DDoS that has hit most relay operators, with relays overwhelmed by a flood of client circuits. The team says this is hard to mitigate, is evaluating more defenses, and expects further releases. Relay operators should upgrade; nothing else is required.

*** Take
The post does not say who is attacking or how the IPv6 fix works, and the team expects more releases, so this is a first patch, not a resolution. The most informative comment is from a home relay operator who only noticed the attack when the household Wi-Fi dropped 99%. Tor's capacity depends on volunteers like that, and an attack that makes running a relay painful also pushes them to quit. Fewer honest relays means a bigger share of the network belongs to whoever stays, so the thread's question about steering traffic to hostile relays is a fair one.

El archivo existe porque el Internet Archive rastreó el sitio durante 30 años sin que nadie se lo pidiera, no porque Salon entregara nada.
