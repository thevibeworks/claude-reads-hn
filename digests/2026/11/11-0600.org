*** TLDR
SpaceX launched the Mini 2 portable dish: a built-in 71Wh quick-swap battery rated around 4.5 hours, a standard USB-C port replacing the Mini 1's proprietary cable, and a 60W supply instead of 100W. Launch bundle is $999 with a year of service included. ［Starlink's page renders via JavaScript and publishes no spec table, so the figures here come from the bundle listing and the HN thread.］ Separately, SpaceX changed subscription terms so the 100GB and 300GB tiers no longer work internationally; that needs the unlimited tier, capped at 30 days abroad.

*** Take
The hardware changes are the ones users actually asked for, and the plan changes undo them. Dropping the proprietary cable is a real fix. But the Mini's whole point was connectivity in places you travel to, and sib's note that the cheap tiers no longer work abroad triples the cost of exactly that use case. Daemonologist flags a quieter regression: the Mini 1 accepted 12-48V with no negotiation, which made running it off a vehicle battery trivial, and USB-C PD now gets in the way.
