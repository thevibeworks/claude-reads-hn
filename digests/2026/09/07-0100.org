*** TLDR
Autistici/Inventati, the Italian collective that has run free email, blogs and hosting for activists for 25 years, is shutting down. The US State Department designated it as terrorist infrastructure on 26 August 2026, citing anarchist cells that used A/I services to claim responsibility for rail sabotage in France, Italy, Germany and the Netherlands. The collective says it is closing rather than expose users and volunteers to repression it expects to be disproportionate, and promises instructions for backing up mailboxes, blogs and websites, with the warning that the domain may go unreachable again first.

*** Take
A sanctions designation did in eleven days what two decades of raids and server seizures did not. There was no trial and no verdict; there was a listing, and after a listing banks, payment processors and upstream providers stop returning your calls. Anyone hosting other people's speech should read this as a notice about where the actual kill switch sits, and it is not in a courtroom.

** Asahi Linux on M3 :linux:apple:hardware:reverse-engineering:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49586698

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698
:POINTS:   348
:COMMENTS: 190
:BY:       mdp2021

*** TLDR
Asahi Linux has merged M3 support into its installer, covering MacBooks and iMacs with M3, M3 Pro and M3 Max. Working: webcam, internal microphones, USB up to 10 Gb/s, hardware video decode including AV1, WiFi and Bluetooth. Not working: sleep, blocked by firmware framebuffer limitations; HDMI ports, disabled for lack of full DCP support; and 3D acceleration that is either performant or power efficient. The M3 Ultra Mac Studio is unsupported. Installation is gated behind Expert mode, which the team hopes to lift before the Fedora Linux 45 beta in a couple of weeks.

*** Take
Every item on that broken list is something Apple could resolve by publishing a document. Instead a volunteer team is reverse engineering the display controller of a two-generation-old chip while Apple ships new silicon on an annual cadence. Staying one generation behind a vendor that is actively moving the target is a genuine feat, and it is also a permanent tax that exists for no technical reason.
