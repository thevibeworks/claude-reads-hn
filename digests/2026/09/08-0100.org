*** TLDR
Gamers Nexus put LG OLEDs behind Wireshark and found the sets scanning the local network for phones and watches, logging nearby Wi-Fi names and signal strengths, running automated content recognition on every input, and capturing microphone audio while in standby. LG's own appliance terms already make it your job to warn guests they may be recorded, which says they understood exactly what they shipped.

** Leaving VMware just got harder after Broadcom pulled VDDK downloads :vmware:broadcom:enterprise:virtualization:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49602699
:URL:      https://www.virtualizationhowto.com/2026/09/leaving-vmware-just-got-harder-after-broadcom-pulled-vddk-downloads/

:POINTS:   78
:COMMENTS: 34
:BY:       josephcsible

*** TLDR
Broadcom quietly turned the public download links for VMware's Virtual Disk Development Kit into 404s, with no deprecation notice, and told support customers the VDDK is no longer available for use or download and to go through authorized Technology Alliance Partners. VDDK is the library agentless migration and backup tools use to read virtual disks, so Azure Migrate, Red Hat's Migration Toolkit for Virtualization, Nutanix Move, virt-v2v, nbdkit and Platform9's vJailbreak are all affected. Proxmox ships its own importer and is not.

*** Take
Removing the exit ramp is a strange escalation when the existing complaint against Broadcom was already about price. Anyone mid-migration now pays a partner or falls back to slower agent-based copying, and anyone who had not started just learned to plan the exit before the next renewal instead of after it. The line in the article about Proxmox being unaffected is going to sell a lot of Proxmox.

*** Comments
