*** TLDR
Cognition factored RSA-260, beating the RSA-250 record from 2020. They built a GPU lattice siever on top of CADO-NFS and ran the general number field sieve on GB200 and GB300 clusters: 4,900 GPU-days, about $400,000 at market rates, three weeks from first prompt to result, and roughly 10x cheaper than the previous public state of the art. Devin did the kernel optimization, parameter tuning and cluster debugging with a human setting goals. They put RSA-1024 at roughly $30 million now, and say RSA-2048, about a billion times harder, is unaffected.

*** Take
The factoring record is real work, but the more interesting claim is the cost curve: 10x cheaper sieving means the number that matters is dollars, not decades. RSA-1024 at $30 million was already a bad bet and is now a worse one. If you still have 1024-bit keys anywhere, this is your notice.
