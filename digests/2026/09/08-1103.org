*** TLDR
Arm announced its first GPU with neural accelerators built into the graphics pipeline, alongside a new execution engine with up to twice the registers per warp and third-generation ray tracing hardware. The concrete claim is up to 24% higher benchmark performance over the previous generation and 14% on non-gaming workloads. Everything larger comes from the neural path: three techniques (Neural Super Sampling, Neural Frame Rate Upscaling, and a combined upscale-and-denoise for ray traced scenes) reach up to 120 FPS and, in Arm's Neural Dawn demo, up to 4x performance efficiency and up to 70% less external memory traffic than native rendering. Hardware Opacity Micromaps cut ray tracing work by up to 70% and lifted frame rates 30% in demos, with 13% lower DRAM traffic on ray tracing benchmarks. No phone maker, chip vendor or ship date is named.

*** Take
The honest number is 24%. Everything past it comes from rendering fewer pixels and inferring the rest, which on a phone is a defensible trade: the 70% cut in external memory traffic matters more than the frame counter, because memory bandwidth is what actually limits mobile GPUs and what actually drains the battery. The thread went straight past the silicon to drivers, which is where Mali has always lost, and an announcement naming no device and no date does not help.

*** TLDR
Revisit: this went into the morning edition with 11 comments and has since passed 100. Australia's draft Digital Duty of Care legislation, published 8 September 2026 and heading to Parliament this year, requires platforms to meet minimum safety standards: shield under-18s from addictive design and from content promoting eating disorders, misogyny, pornography, crime and self-harm, document their risk mitigations, and pull nudify apps when the eSafety Commissioner asks. The part with the name, My Feed My Way, is a prompt to users 16 and over to choose their default feed between algorithmic recommendations and posts only from accounts they follow. The duty applies to online games, apps, messaging and AI chatbots as well as social networks, with penalties up to A$109.2 million.

*** Take
The feed prompt is the headline and the smallest part of the bill. The operative clause is the duty of care itself: a A$109.2 million penalty attached to safety obligations reaching online games, messaging and AI chatbots, which makes this a chatbot regulation arriving inside a story about algorithm toggles. Nearly the whole thread argues about whether people will switch their feed back, and they probably will, while the part that changes what a company has to build gets no attention at all.

***** Title
Por qué ensuciarse las manos es bueno para ti
***** TLDR
BBC Future repasa investigaciones sobre contacto con el suelo, en su mayoría finlandesas. En el experimento de Grönroos, frotarse las manos con tierra, turba y musgo durante 20 segundos aumentó la abundancia y la variedad de microbios de la piel, y el efecto sobrevivió a un enjuague de grifo, pero la piel volvió pronto a algo cercano al punto de partida, así que hay que repetirlo para que cuente. El ensayo de guardería de Roslund consistió en 28 días de juego sobre suelo forestal con seguimiento un año después: esos niños tenían más diversidad microbiana en la piel y menos patógenos potenciales que los de patios pavimentados, y a mayor diversidad de gammaproteobacterias, más células T reguladoras. Un ensayo de arenero comparando tierra microbianamente diversa con tierra pobre, un mes de adultos cultivando verduras en interior y dos semanas con muros verdes de oficina apuntaron en la misma dirección: más diversidad bacteriana en la piel, más citocinas antiinflamatorias, menos proinflamatorias y más Lactobacillus. Los investigadores dicen con claridad que sigue sin estar claro si esto reduce el asma o las alergias.
***** Take
Los marcadores inmunitarios son mediciones reales, y los estudios son pequeños y finlandeses, que es la frase que debería seguir a todo titular con esta forma. El hallazgo más útil está enterrado en el ensayo del arenero: la comparación era tierra diversa contra tierra pobre, no tierra contra nada. Si eso se sostiene, el suelo agotado de una jardinera urbana puede hacer muy poco, y el consejo no es ensuciarse sino acercarse a suelo vivo.
